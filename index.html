Research Statement

About & core interests

"My thesis applies panel-data regression in STATA to examine how governance mechanisms, institutional ownership, and sustainability reporting shape firm performance in Pakistan's non-financial sector."

I am a research-oriented finance professional with applied expertise in corporate governance, financial econometrics, and empirical valuation. My MBA thesis investigates how board-level governance mechanisms and sustainability disclosure moderate corporate return on assets across Pakistan's listed non-financial sector, using fixed- and random-effects panel regression.

This is complemented by independent econometric and valuation work, including CAPM regression and volatility analysis across five PSX-listed equities, and FCFF-based DCF valuation and three-statement modelling for firms across the oil & gas and FMCG sectors. I am seeking doctoral opportunities in finance, accounting, or economics to extend this empirical research into underexplored emerging-market settings.

The Nexus Between Corporate Governance and Corporate Financial Performance

2026
MBA Thesis · In Progress

Evaluates board-level governance mechanisms — audit committee independence, institutional ownership — across Pakistan's non-financial listed sector, with sustainability/ESG reporting modelled as a moderator of return on assets. Fixed- and random-effects panel regression in STATA, with diagnostic checks for multicollinearity, heteroskedasticity, and autocorrelation. Supervised by Dr. Yasir Ashraf.

CAPM & Volatility Analysis of PSX-Listed Equities

Mar 2026
Financial Econometrics · MBA609

CAPM regressions (simple and log returns) for five PSX-listed equities — LUCK, OGDC, PSO, FFC, HBL — against the KSE-100 benchmark over June 2024–May 2026. Includes Breusch-Pagan heteroskedasticity and Breusch-Godfrey autocorrelation diagnostics, and an evaluation of the sample against the weak-form Efficient Market Hypothesis.
